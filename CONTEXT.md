## 3. The Social Impact Ecosystem (12-Stage Cycle)

The operational engine runs across an infinite, regenerative 12-stage cycle:
$$\text{IDENTIFY} \to \text{ENGAGE} \to \text{ASSESS} \to \text{CONNECT} \to \text{CO-DESIGN} \to \text{MOBILISE} \to \text{ACTIVATE} \to \text{MEASURE} \to \text{LEARN} \to \text{ADAPT} \to \text{SCALE} \to \text{REINVEST} \to \text{(CYCLE REPEATS)}$$

1. **IDENTIFIES:** People, Social Issues, Assets, Influence, Opportunities, Risks, Resources.
2. **ENGAGES:** Celebrated Individuals, Groups, Communities, Institutions, Social Entrepreneurs, Partners.
3. **LISTENS & ASSESSES:** Needs, Strengths, Risks, Capacities, Relationships, Contexts, Resources.
4. **CONNECTS:** People, Problems, Expertise, Influence, Networks, Technology, Markets, Funding.
5. **CO-DESIGNS:** Ideas, Interventions, Enterprises, Campaigns, Partnerships, Community Solutions.
6. **MOBILISES:** People, Capital, Knowledge, Technology, Networks, Infrastructure, Opportunities.
7. **ACTIVATES:** Social Work, Entrepreneurship, Advocacy, Innovation, Research, Community Action.
8. **MEASURES & LEARNS:** Outcomes, Impact, Inclusion, Value, Sustainability, Evidence, Lessons.
9. **ADAPTS:** What works, what changes, what communities need, what emerging evidence reveals.
10. **SCALES SOCIAL VALUE:** Replicate, Adapt, Institutionalize, Partner, Invest, Influence Systems.
11. **REINVESTS:** In people, communities, and new solutions.
12. **REGENERATIVE ENGINE:** Perpetually feeds all value and credibility back into Stage 01.
